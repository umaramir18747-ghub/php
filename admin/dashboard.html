Help & Guidance

Dashboard Overview: Use this dashboard to monitor the overall status of students, teachers, classes, and fee records. The statistics are updated automatically from the information entered throughout the portal.

Admission Details: Check total students and teachers, active classes and students, and graduated students and classes. Use the Students, Classes, and Teachers quick links above to view detailed records.

Fee Details: Monitor paid and unpaid payments, overdue payments, total amount received, remaining amount, and the overall fee amount. Due Payments represent unpaid fee records whose due date has passed.

Reports: Use the Reports option to review and generate useful information from the school's records for monitoring and decision-making.

Automatic Updates: Dashboard statistics refresh when the page is opened and when you return to the dashboard, helping you keep track of the latest available records.
